## Stat Pumping Priority

Mechanician with Claws is a **balanced melee build**, requiring even investment
across STR, TAL, and AGI. Claws demand moderate values in all three combat stats.
SPI is required for Rings, Amulets, and Sheltoms — the only SPI-gated slots.

| Priority | Stat | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **STR** | Required by Armor, Shield, Boots, Gauntlets, Bracelets. Armor STR outpaces claws. Scales from 52→248. |
| 2 | **TAL** | Required by claws, armor, shields, gauntlets. Claw TAL scales 0→97. Caps at 90 from level 90, then jumps to 95–97 at 110+. |
| 3 | **AGI** | Required by claws, Boots, Gauntlets, Bracelets. Claw AGI caps at 82 from level 60 onward. Bracelets/Boots push it to 105 at 113. |
| 4 | **SPI** | Required for Rings, Amulets, Sheltoms. Some claws need SPI (Leviathan, Chaos Claw, Chimera Talon). Scales from 28→110. |

## Stat Allocation Guide (per level-up)

- **Levels 1–29:** Pump STR for armor/shield. Keep TAL and AGI at claw minimums (30–44). Keep SPI at ~28–32 for early rings/amulets.
- **Levels 30–54:** STR still dominant (74→120, armor-driven). TAL moderate (~48–70). AGI climbs slowly (~50–61).
- **Levels 55–90:** STR climbs to 208 (armor). TAL caps at 90 from level 90. AGI caps at 82 (claws) then edges up from boots/gauntlets/bracelets to 90. SPI rises to 85.
- **Levels 90+:** STR reaches 248 at 110 (mythology armor). TAL jumps to 95–97 at 110+. AGI edges to 105 from bracelets. SPI reaches 110 at 108.
