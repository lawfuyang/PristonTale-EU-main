## Stat Pumping Priority

Fighter is a **STR-primary melee class**. All gear scales with STR.
TAL is needed for axes, armor, and shields — axes demand moderate TAL.
AGI is required for Boots, Gauntlets, and Bracelets. Axes do not need AGI (only Jagged Axe at 55 requires AGI:50).
SPI is required for Rings, Amulets, and Sheltoms — the only SPI-gated slots.

| Priority | Stat | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **STR** | Required by every equip slot: axe, shield, armor, boots, gauntlets, bracelets. Largest gap between milestones. Scales from 52→248. |
| 2 | **TAL** | Required by axes, armor, shield, gauntlets. Axe TAL scales 0→90, then jumps to 100 at 110+. Caps at 90 from level 65 onward. |
| 3 | **AGI** | Required by Boots, Gauntlets, Bracelets. Axes barely need AGI. Scales from 30→105 (driven by boots/bracelets). |
| 4 | **SPI** | Required for Rings, Amulets, Sheltoms. Scales from 28→110. |

## Stat Allocation Guide (per level-up)

- **Levels 1–29:** Pump STR heavily → TAL. Keep TAL at axe minimums (30–50). Minimal AGI (~30–44) for boots/bracelets. Keep SPI at ~28–32 for early rings/amulets.
- **Levels 30–54:** STR still dominant (86→120). TAL moderate (~50–70). AGI stays moderate (~50–58).
- **Levels 55–90:** STR climbs to 208 (armor-driven). TAL caps at 90 from level 65. AGI plateaus at 90 from level 75 (boots/bracelets). SPI rises to 85.
- **Levels 90+:** STR continues to 248 (mythology armor). TAL reaches 100 at 113 (Abyss Axe). AGI edges to 105 from bracelets. SPI reaches 110 at 108.
