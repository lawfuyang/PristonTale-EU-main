## Stat Pumping Priority

Archer is an **AGI-primary ranged class**. Bows demand massive AGI investment.
STR and TAL are needed for Armor and shared melee slots.
SPI is required for Rings, Amulets, and Sheltoms — the only SPI-gated slots.

| Priority | Stat | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **AGI** | Primary bow stat. Scales from 36→225, far outpacing all others. Largest gap between milestones. |
| 2 | **STR** | Required by Armor (and some high-level bows). Armor STR scales from 52→248. |
| 3 | **TAL** | Required by bows and armor throughout. Caps at 90 from level 55–108, then jumps to 95–97 at 110+. |
| 4 | **SPI** | Required for Rings, Amulets, Sheltoms. No combat gear needs SPI. Scales from 28→110. |

## Stat Allocation Guide (per level-up)

- **Levels 1–29:** Pump AGI → STR. Keep TAL at minimum for bows (24–50). Keep SPI at ~28–32 for early rings/amulets.
- **Levels 30–54:** AGI still the dominant stat (65→90). STR rises to ~120 (armor). TAL moderate (~50–70).
- **Levels 55–89:** AGI skyrockets (95→150). STR rises to ~184. TAL caps at 90 from level 55. SPI rises to ~80.
- **Levels 90+:** AGI continues to 225. STR reaches 248 at 110. TAL stays at 90 until 110+ where it jumps to 95–97. SPI reaches 110 at 108.
